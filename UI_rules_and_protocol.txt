SYSTEM SPECIFICATION & EVALUATION CRITERIA
What a Complete Response Should Cover
All participating teams are evaluated on whether their autonomous intelligence agent and user interface address the complete 9-stage restoration protocol.

Ground rule for every scenario
The survivor has no instruments and no lab. Your System must not ask for, quote, or depend on scientific readings or fixed numbers. Any check it suggests must be doable with eyes, hands, and household items, and amounts must be described in everyday terms (a handful, a bucketful, a pace, a hand's depth).

STEP 01
INPUT ECHO
Diagnostic Echo & Cue Grounding
Restates the land description and image cues accurately (soil condition, cracking, puddles, crust, heat, rainfall, vegetation) without hallucinating or inventing details that were not given.

STEP 02
MISSING ANALYSIS
Soil Archetype & Root Failure Diagnosis
Identifies the specific soil archetype and diagnoses root causes — names what is missing (drainage, moisture retention, structure, organic matter, canopy/windbreak) and isolates dominant failure factors.

STEP 03
FIX PROTOCOL
Quantified Remediation & Sequencing
Delivers quantified, step-by-step stabilization and remediation measures (e.g. soil amendments per m², mulch depth, leaching cycles, loosening depth, channel specs) strictly prior to crop selection.

STEP 04
NEXT ACTION
Empirical Readiness Gate
Establishes an empirical verification checkpoint that must pass before planting begins (e.g. soil moisture retention test, infiltration timing, crumb test, EC test) with clear pass/fail thresholds.

STEP 05
CROP SUGGESTIONS
Rationalized Resilient Crop Options
Offers multiple genuinely resilient crop options rationalized against the specific site signals (not a generic list), structured so the user can compare trade-offs and ecological advantages.

STEP 06
TIMEFRAME
Dual Timeline & Remediation Buffer
Provides realistic timeline accounting for both remediation buffer / soil recovery and true crop maturity under environmental stress (expressed as a realistic range, not a textbook single number).

STEP 07
PLANTING METHOD
Substrate Prep & Matched Irrigation
Specifies tailored bed/substrate preparation, planting depth/geometry, and an irrigation setup matched to scarce water or drainage constraints (e.g., drip, clay pot, mulched basin, raised bed).

STEP 08
ONGOING CARE
Staged Cultivation & Stress Mitigation
Multi-stage cultivation regimen covering organic fertility, abiotic stress mitigation (heat/wind/salt/waterlogging), pest & pathogen surveillance, and biological intervention options.

STEP 09
HARVEST & REGEN
Harvest Signs & Soil Regeneration
Clear ripeness indicators, harvesting protocol, and post-harvest soil regenerative practices (cover cropping, residue recycling, preserving windbreaks/beds) so land degradation does not restart.

Core System & User Experience Rules
📐
Clear Step-by-Step UI Sequencing
The participant's system interface must clearly present soil preparation and readiness testing before crop recommendations. Order of execution is critical.

🛡️
Zero Hallucinations
Every diagnostic observation and recommendation must be grounded in the provided environmental profile and image cues. Do not assume unstated factors.

🔄
Generalization Across Conditions
The system must handle the full Range of Conditions within its scenario category, not just one memorized test sample. Flexibility under variance is tested.
